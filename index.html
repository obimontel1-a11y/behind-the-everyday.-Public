My story

I’m a student nurse, and my experience of studying and navigating challenges made me curious about how differently people work. Things I found difficult sometimes seemed easier for others, and I didn’t always understand why.

As I began to understand how I process information and respond to challenges, I found approaches that suited me better. That experience inspired me to create an opportunity for other people to explore themselves, in their own words and at their own pace.

What is the goal?

To help you develop a clearer understanding of yourself and use it to choose meaningful, realistic goals. By the end of the series you’ll have:

✦ a personal map of what you’ve discovered
✦ an understanding of what supports your progress
✦ a small next step that fits your life

A question to get you started

“If you came with an instruction manual, what’s one thing it would say?”

Not sure? Here’s mine: “Give me time to process things before expecting an answer.” That’s the sort of thing this project explores: what we notice about ourselves and how we can use it in everyday life.
